Welcome Note

It is my great pleasure to welcome you to the Jubail Corrosion Conference (JUBCOR).

Corrosion remains one of the most critical challenges facing industrial sectors worldwide, particularly in energy, petrochemicals, infrastructure, and manufacturing. Its impact on asset integrity, safety, reliability, and lifecycle costs makes effective corrosion management a strategic priority rather than a purely technical concern.

JUBCOR has been established as a dedicated platform to bring together asset owners, operators, engineers, researchers, and technology providers to exchange knowledge, share practical experiences, and explore innovative solutions in corrosion prevention, monitoring, and control. Hosting this conference in Jubail Industrial City—one of the world's most important industrial hubs—underscores the relevance and urgency of the discussions taking place.

Through technical presentations, case studies, and expert dialogue, the conference aims to highlight emerging technologies, promote industry best practices, and encourage collaboration across disciplines and organizations. We are particularly focused on advancing sustainable, reliable, and cost-effective approaches to corrosion management that support long-term operational excellence.

I would like to extend my sincere appreciation to our speakers, sponsors, partners, and delegates for their valuable contributions and commitment to advancing this important field. Your participation is what makes JUBCOR a meaningful and impactful event.

I wish you a productive and rewarding conference, and I look forward to the insightful discussions and collaborations that will emerge over the course of the event.

Dr. Fahad Al-Mutlaq Conference Chairman
